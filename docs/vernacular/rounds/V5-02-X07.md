## Dispatch 2 (2026-10-28, coordinator): repair of the ledger sidecar race

### Objective

The X07 candidate (waves A and B) is applied in the working tree. The gate failed twice. Operator decision 2026-10-28: repair inside X07, with scope widened to `src/engine/ledger/storage.cpp` and a ledger-level regression test.

### Diagnosis

`storage.cpp` calls `no_sidecars()` (line 235, which refuses with `recovery_required` when `<ledger>-journal`, `-wal` or `-shm` exists) before this connection holds the SQLite write lock: at about line 1364 before `state->db.begin()` (BEGIN IMMEDIATE), and on the open path at about lines 1158 and 1182 before the connection is opened. In journal mode DELETE a concurrent winner's live `-journal` file legitimately exists during its commit, so the loser treats a live writer's journal as a crash leftover.

### Required behaviour (ledger contract)

Per `docs/vernacular/ledger-contract.md`: busy wait of 5000 ms with typed `ledger_busy`, BEGIN IMMEDIATE, then revision compare-and-swap giving `revision_conflict`. Hot-journal crash recovery applies only to a recognized owned v1 database (contract lines about 485 to 498). A sidecar observed while another connection may hold the lock must not refuse as `recovery_required`. Sidecars are checked only once this connection holds the lock (after BEGIN IMMEDIATE, or inside the read transaction on the read path). A sidecar that persists under the held lock still refuses where the contract requires it. `format.cpp` uses of `no_sidecars` for init and staging stay unchanged unless the same race applies; any change there must be justified in the record.

### Amendment to the Dispatch 2 prescription (deliberate, needs reviewer judgment)

Dispatch 2 prescribed checking sidecars only after BEGIN IMMEDIATE. The repair keeps the early checks (open path and before BEGIN IMMEDIATE)
and adds the bounded grace poll instead. Reason given by the repair coder: a live winner's `-journal` exists only during its commit, so a
bounded wait removes the false refusal while a persistent sidecar still refuses. Cost: a genuine crash-leftover sidecar delays open and
begin_write by about 1 s before refusing (existing refusal tests run slower). Whether a 1 s grace is within the ledger contract's busy-wait and
hot-journal clauses is not established by the gate or probes. Consequence: the three write-path `no_sidecars` calls remain mutually redundant for
every test (survivors M09, M10, M12 below). Original acceptance criterion "sidecar check after the lock" of Dispatch 2 is therefore not implemented as written.
