## Independent finding

Fresh reviewer `/root/r13b1_reviewer` reproduced a snapshot-lifetime defect:
an injected real SQLite read failure rolls back the pinned transaction, but
`format_file::read_record` leaves its owner usable. A subsequent call can then
observe a newly committed record through an implicit newer snapshot. The
unchanging reader must instead become unavailable after loss of its snapshot.
Preserve the original candidate and reproduction; a fresh corrective round
must resolve the finding before B2 advances.
