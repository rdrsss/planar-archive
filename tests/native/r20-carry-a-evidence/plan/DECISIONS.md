| D17'' | Embed helper takes the single write. Grammar: `planar_embed_lua(target module source parts... [REMAP <abs> <logical> ...] [LITERAL <from> <to> ...])`. Parts end at the first token equal to `REMAP` or `LITERAL`. REMAP pairs: (abs path, logical name); a bare `REMAP` with zero pairs is legal and a no-op. Names are computed with the helper's OWN rule: `file(RELATIVE_PATH l "${PROJECT_SOURCE_DIR}/src/stdlib" <abs>)`; if `l` starts with `..` then `file(RELATIVE_PATH l "${PROJECT_SOURCE_DIR}" <abs>)`; then `string(REPLACE "${l}" "<logical>" content "${content}")`.
